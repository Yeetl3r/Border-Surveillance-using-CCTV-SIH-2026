02CAPABILITY

What it sees, and what it will stand behind

Five object classes. Eight event kinds. Every one of them routed through the same temporal confirmation, and none of them shown to an operator until the system is willing to prove why.

CLASS 01
Person
15–25 px tall at 150 m on wide-angle CCTV. SAHI tiling recovers them.
CLASS 02
Vehicle
Plate localisation on the crop, then PP-OCRv6 tuned for HSRP.
CLASS 03
Animal
The canonical false alarm. Excluded per-zone by configuration, not by luck.
CLASS 04
Bag
Associated to an owner in metres. Never seen with a person means orphan, not abandoned.
CLASS 05
Aerial
One frame cannot separate a drone from a bird. Flight path over many frames can.
CRITICAL WHEN UNIDENTIFIED
Perimeter crossing
A directional tripwire in ground metres. Crossing needs a sign change in the signed-area test and an intersection with the finite segment — walking past the end of a fence is not a breach.
F4 · SHAPELY · INFILTRATION ≠ EXFILTRATION
WARNING
Zone presence
Point-in-polygon containment against restricted areas, evaluated on the footpoint after homography. Each zone declares which object groups it even tests.
F4 · CONFIRMS ONCE, NOT PER FRAME
WARNING
Proximity breach
Euclidean metres to a protected asset. Three metres is three metres at the near edge of frame and at the far end of the scene; a pixel threshold is wrong in at least one of them.
F5 · DEPTH-INVARIANT
WARNING
Unattended object
Accompanied → detached → dwell timer → alert, with a reclaim grace period. Bag-to-owner radius and separation are in metres. 15 s on stage, 120 s in the field.
F6 · ORPHAN BAGS DO NOT ALERT
WATCH
Infiltration stance
Torso-axis verticality from 17 COCO keypoints on the person crop — not bounding-box aspect ratio, which climbs from 0.39 to 0.84 purely from frame truncation and reads a walking person as crawling.
F16 · EDGE-CLIPPED FRAMES SUPPRESSED
FRIENDLY / UNIDENTIFIED
Identity, open-set
Matched, not matched, or could not tell. Unidentified is a statement about enrolment, never about intent. There is no HOSTILE value in the contract.
F8 · OSNet · QUALITY-GATED
CRITICAL ON HOVER
Aerial contact
Payload UAV seizures on the Punjab border: 107 in 2023, 294 in 2024, 272 plus 367 kg of heroin in 2025. Path linearity, hover, turn radius and acceleration separate quadcopter from bird.
F17 · MULTI-FRAME TRAJECTORY
WARNING
Camera tamper
Laplacian variance, SSIM drift against a slowly-updated reference, and bit-identical frame detection. Adversaries blind cameras before crossing; "nothing is happening" and "I have been blinded" are different reports.
F9 · 0.20 s · NO MODEL REQUIRED

Two values that must never be conflated

How serious it would be, and how certain the system is. Threat drives colour; confidence drives line style. A low-confidence CRITICAL is a red hatched box — this would be serious, and I am not certain. Multiply them into one number and the operator can no longer tell whether the system is unsure or unconcerned.

03STACK & MEASUREMENT

Every number measured on named hardware

RTX 5080, PyTorch 2.11+cu128, Python 3.13. Source footage 1280×720, 15 fps, monochrome IR at roughly 97 kbps — a bitrate at which compression artefacts are visible in every frame. Nothing here is carried over from a specification sheet.

DETECTION · AGPL-3.0
YOLO26-s
12.4 ms/frame
NMS-free end to end, DFL removed, exported through TensorRT at INT8. STAL label assignment guarantees positive-sample coverage for small objects — which is the defining difficulty of this footage. SAHI tiles the frame at native scale where targets sit beyond 150 m.
TRACKING · MIT
BoT-SORT
9.1 ms/frame
Best IDF1 and lowest identity-switch count in published comparisons; appearance-aware association is what survives fence and vegetation occlusion. One instance per camera — shared tracker state cross-contaminates identities. ByteTrack and FastTracker stay selectable in YAML.
GEOMETRY · OPENCV + SHAPELY
4-point homography
0.0000 m residual
A ground plane maps to an image by an exact homography, so the round-trip residual must be zero. A non-trivial residual means the marked points were not coplanar — someone clicked the top of a wall. The system reports the residual instead of concealing it.
IDENTITY · MIT
OSNet x0.25
0.666 threshold, fitted
2.31 M parameters, 512-d embedding, four depthwise streams fused by a learned gate. The ReLU head puts every embedding in the positive orthant, so unrelated crops already sit at 0.4–0.6 cosine. Same-subject median 0.8941 (n=780) against negative p95 0.5564 (n=2,400) — the threshold is measured, not chosen by feel.
REASONING · NVIDIA OPEN MODEL
LocateAnything-3B
620 ms – 1.5 s / keyframe
Parallel box decoding, 12.7 boxes/second against 1.1 for textual decoding, 9.84 GB peak VRAM. Invoked on breach or operator request only. Queue depth is one, with rejection — a three-billion-parameter model in a video loop is an architectural error, and refusal under load is the feature.
RETRIEVAL · POSTGRES + MILVUS
Query router
0.06 – 7.5 ms structured
Four routes: SQL under 5 ms, identity vectors under 20 ms, archive clip index around 100 ms, frame grounding async. "When was the last threat found?" is a SQL query, not a VLM invocation four orders of magnitude slower. Operator language selects a parameterised template and never becomes SQL.
0.20 s
From lens obscuration to a raised tamper alert, with no model in the path.
-- B
Worst-case tactical relay against a 3,072-byte budget. The image degrades to fit; the JSON is never truncated.
48 tests · 1.29 s
Suppression policy verified with no GPU, no video and no model weights — because contracts import nothing.
Zero
Custom models trained for the core perimeter stack. Two fully-permissive deployment configurations exist.
04ARCHITECTURE

Nine tiers, swappable interfaces, one workstation

Each tier speaks only through typed contracts. Every boundary that crosses a speed mismatch is a bounded queue with a counted, displayed drop policy — unbounded queues between video rate and a multi-second model are the single most likely cause of a live failure under load.

T5
Identity orchestrator
Open-set by construction. A quality gate embeds only crops that can support a claim — minimum pixels, minimum sharpness, no edge truncation — because a 30-pixel blurred crop produces an embedding that is confidently wrong. Identity accrues over a track's lifetime, so one bad frame never downgrades a verdict. Local track IDs resolve to a global entity ID across cameras.
